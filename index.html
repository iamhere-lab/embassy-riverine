About the Project

About Embassy Riverine

Embassy Riverine is the villa-home collection of Embassy Origins, an 85.5-acre, planning-led and nature-first township in north Yelahanka. The entire landscape is planned as a single continuous environment, where villa homes and eco apartments each keep their own identity and rhythm of living within one ecological address.

The villa homes are planned for lower density, stronger privacy and a more expansive indoor-outdoor way of living. Every home is positioned to engage with the riparian corridors, and backyards extend until their edges dissolve into the landscape.

Composed in accordance with the golden ratio from north to south, the master plan brings together native tree cover, ecological buffers and over 5.37 crore litres of on-site water storage, for better air quality and lower temperatures, close to the city yet closer to nature.

Prime Location

North Yelahanka is a nature-friendly residential area that sits away from the noise of the city, yet is well connected to its key destinations. Embassy Riverine sits on Chapparkallu Road, between NH 44 and the Doddaballapura Main Road, about 20 minutes from Kempegowda International Airport.
